## 2. Contradictions and limitations found (reported before implementation)

1. **UK data cannot come from Eurostat for a commercial game.** Eurostat excludes non-EU, non-EFTA, non-candidate countries from commercial reuse, and holds UK data only to 2019. The UK must use ONS (Open Government Licence v3), which is available to 2024. This is consistent but means a second source pipeline.
2. **Türkiye has three gaps in Eurostat and OECD:**
   - spending by function (COFOG);
   - Maastricht debt (Eurostat only);
   - regional employment.

   TurkStat publishes the first two (licence verified; years still to confirm). Regional employment needs TurkStat's Labour Force Survey (not verified).
3. **Military personnel: no open, licence-verified source for all six yet.** SIPRI and IISS-derived data can't be bundled commercially. Expenditure is covered by open COFOG data. The approved "abstract forces" direction is fine, but its scaling formula needs either a verified personnel source (NATO's report is the candidate, licence unverified) or expenditure only (decision D-M1).
4. **Natural Earth draws Crimea and Sevastopol inside Russia.** Following the territorial principle requires a distinct "disputed" map style (a rendering task, Codex's area). Without it, the default map takes a political position.
5. **Poland's entire eastern border is bloc territory** (Ukraine, Belarus, Kaliningrad), and so is Türkiye's eastern and southern border. These neighbours have no diplomacy or military in v1. That is a deliberate simplification, but it limits interaction on those borders.
6. **France's economic totals must exclude the overseas departments.** Eurostat's French national totals include them, so France's numbers must be built from its metropolitan provinces to avoid inconsistency.
7. **Performance outliers.** At 6× slowdown the slowest single week reached 0.71 s at 60 zones and 0.96 s at 75. The median and 95th percentile are well within target. A device measurement must confirm before 60 zones is final.
8. **Concept gaps** between official data and the engine (`WORLD-02-DATA-SOURCES.md` §4) need owner choices rather than measurement:
   - social contributions;
   - other taxes;
   - military industry location;
   - mining vs energy split at regional level.

## 4. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| iPhone slower than the Chromium proxy | medium | high | Device test before any scenario build; 49-zone fallback |
| Real calibration harder or slower than the synthetic benchmark | medium | medium | Re-benchmark on the calibrated world; report residuals; never invent parameters |
| TurkStat series missing or incompatible for 2023 | medium | medium | Nearest-year scaling rule (documented estimate) |
| Licence change at a publisher | low | high | Licence check repeated at each data refresh; provenance file per value |
| Territorial labels perceived as political | medium | high | Neutral, source-based wording; owner review; one data file |
| Scope creep from "simulated" to "playable" neighbours | medium | medium | Each promotion is a separately approved task with its own zone budget |

## 5. Verification done in this milestone

- **Licence pages read directly:** Natural Earth, Eurostat, ONS and the OGL, INSEE, Destatis, ISTAT, GUS, TurkStat, the World Bank, EIA, FAO and SIPRI.
- **Licences found through search only:** OECD and IMF, whose pages block automated access.
- **Data-year queries run against the live APIs:** Eurostat for 11 datasets across 6 countries, and OECD availability for Revenue Statistics and COFOG Table 11.
- **Province mapping** generated from the repository's Natural Earth files: 556 of 556 units assigned. Flags cover overseas departments, mixed admin levels, spelling differences, reform-era Italian provinces and the split voivodeship.
- **Membership partition:** 258 of 258 entities, each exactly once (checked by the script).
- **Benchmark:** 5 worlds in Node and in Chromium at 1×, 4× and 6×, on the current `main` engine. An earlier run on the pre-ECON-07 engine gave matching figures (the 60-zone Node median was 44 ms then and 45 ms now).
- **No engine, game, UI or geography source was changed.** Engine and game test suites: see the PR.
