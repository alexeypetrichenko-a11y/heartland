**Recommendation: option A, 61 zones.** 60 and 61 zones cannot be told apart:
- **Node:** run-to-run noise (medians 44.8–46.7 ms) is as large as the gap between them.
- **Chromium at 6×:** one world's two runs differ by up to 4 ms.

One zone changes cost by only 1–2%, so option B gives up a strategic actor (Norway, or the separation between the Americas and Asia) for no measurable saving.

**At 6× Chromium, option A meets every F-1 threshold.** The closest is the slowest week, at 0.71–0.77 s against a 1 s limit.

**If the iPhone fails,** removing one zone will not fix it: go to option C, which saves about a quarter (median 103 ms at 6×).

**Memory:**
- Node heap: 15–17 MB.
- Chromium JS heap: 36–82 MB.

**Runs:**
- Node: 3 runs of 155 weeks per world.
- Chromium: 2 runs per world at 1×, 4× and 6×.

Desktop and Chromium figures are measurements. **The iPhone has not been measured.** `WORLD-02-PERFORMANCE.md` §4 gives the procedure, now using the 61-zone real-topology page.

## 4. PPP analysis (D2), in short

- **The engine's money works like one purchasing-power unit.** In a measured experiment, a threefold productivity gap gives only a 10% price-level gap, whereas in reality Türkiye's 2023 price level was 41% of Germany's. **Calibrating real output at PPP is therefore the consistent choice.**
- **Share of the six-country economy, market rates → PPP:**

  | | Market rates | PPP |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | Germany | 29.8% | 25.7% |
  | UK | 22.3% | 17.8% |
  | France | 20.0% | 17.8% |
  | Italy | 15.1% | 15.2% |
  | Türkiye | **7.5%** | **15.8%** |
  | Poland | 5.3% | 7.6% |

  Source: World Bank WDI 2023, cross-checked against Eurostat; every figure verified.
- **Nominal government finances** (taxes, spending, debt, treasury) enter as ratios to GDP measured in national currency, so they are independent of the currency choice. **Money conservation is unaffected**, because the conversion happens once, offline.
- **Market-rate GDP** is shown only as static reference text.
- **Limitation:** external purchasing power, meaning a weak currency that makes imports expensive, cannot be represented without national currencies. That is mechanic R36, which is **not** proposed. The smallest mitigation is to calibrate trade openness as a ratio and report the residual.

## 5. Military proposal (D3), in short

- **Rule W:** divisions = round((defence pay ÷ the country's own average pay per employee) ÷ 25,000). The only assumption is the 25,000 game scale.
- **2023 result:**

  | France | Germany | UK | Italy | Poland | Türkiye |
  |---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
  | 15 | 13 | 14 | 15 | 13 | **data gap** |

  Total for the five with data: 70 divisions.
- **Why not spending at market rates:** it would give Poland 5 divisions and the UK 23. This is the high-wage bias. PPP spending (Rule P) gives 8 and 20 and is kept as a cross-check.
- **Limitations:**
  - Conscripts are undercounted.
  - COFOG defence is not NATO's definition.
  - Türkiye has no open data.
  - Divisions have only `manpower`.
  - Engine military spending does not maintain divisions.

  So only the **starting count** can come from data, and placement stays illustrative.

## 10. Proposed next milestone: WORLD-03, the scenario data pipeline (still no playable scenario)

**Goal:** produce the first **calibrated, versioned European scenario data file** and a **report**, without wiring it into the game.

**Scope:**
1. Extract the 2023 values listed in `DATA-SOURCES.md` §2–7 for the 61 zones, each with a provenance record.
2. Fit `technology` and `advantage` offline under the §5 tolerances.
3. Produce the calibration report.
4. Benchmark the calibrated world.
5. Write the save-versioning specification.

S-1 (sea-link input) is included only if the owner approves it.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Every value in the scenario file has a provenance entry (source, table code, year, access date, licence, transformation). Only ✅ sources (§6) are used, and no value is invented. Estimates are flagged with their method.
- The validator passes on the scenario's zones and links: exactly-once crosswalk, partition, connectivity, and Corsica with no land link.
- The calibration meets the §5 tolerances, or every residual is listed for the owner:
  - province GVA share ±3%;
  - country GDP share (PPP) ±3%;
  - tax, spending and debt ratios ±1 percentage point.
- The engine is unchanged: the existing engine tests and the golden fingerprint pass unchanged, and the game test suite and production build pass.
- The calibrated 61-zone world is benchmarked in Node and Chromium (1×, 4×, 6×), and the iPhone page is regenerated from it. Results are reported against F-1.
- The scenario has an ID, a version and a fingerprint. A short spec describes how SAVE-01 will check them (for Codex).
- Review-only: there are no UI, game-rule or gameplay changes, and nothing is merged without approval.
