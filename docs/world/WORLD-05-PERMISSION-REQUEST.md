# WORLD-05 — Draft permission request (route A, decision W5-L1)

**Status:** draft for the owner to review, complete and send. Nothing has been sent.
- **Fill in before sending:** the sender's details and the game's public name.
- **Addressee:** the unit the Ministry's own table metadata names as responsible for these statistics, the Ministry of Treasury and Finance, General Directorate of Public Accounts, Fiscal Statistics Department.
- **Channels:** a written application through CİMER or the Ministry's e-application or e-mail channel. Applications under the Right to Information Law (No. 4982) are answered within 15 working days, or 30 when another unit must be consulted (Art. 11, read on mevzuat.gov.tr 2026-11-25). A permission request may not fall under that law, so treat the deadline as indicative. A copy may also go to the Court of Accounts for the 2023 evaluation report.
- **Wording:** this is not legal wording. The owner may want a lawyer to review it before relying on the answer.

**Subject:** Request for permission to reuse and redistribute General Government Fiscal Statistics and Central Government Budget Statistics

Republic of Türkiye Ministry of Treasury and Finance
General Directorate of Public Accounts, Fiscal Statistics Department

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am [name / role], developing *[game name]*, a strategy game played in a web browser. Its European scenario takes each country's 2023 economic starting values from official statistics.

For Türkiye, we would like to use **only a few aggregate values** from the following publications of your Ministry:

1. *General Government Sector Fiscal Statistics 2008–2026* (https://muhasebat.hmb.gov.tr), 2023, general government:
   - revenue;
   - expense: compensation of employees, goods and services, consumption of fixed capital, interest, subsidies, grants, social benefits, other;
   - net investment in non-financial assets;
   - net lending/borrowing;
   - defence (702) in the functional classification of outlays.
2. *Central Government Budget Expenditure Outturn 2023, by function and economic type (December, cumulative)*: personnel expenditure and state social security contributions in the defence row.
3. *Public Debt Management Report*: EU-defined general government gross debt at the end of 2023.

These values will appear in the game's scenario data files as ratios to GDP, adapted (transformed) to the game's economic model. The source will be stated clearly in every distribution: "Source: Republic of Türkiye Ministry of Treasury and Finance, General Directorate of Public Accounts".

We kindly ask for your written permission or opinion on:
- (a) reproducing and distributing these values, and values derived from them, with the source cited, in the data files of a game that may be distributed free of charge or for a fee, **including commercial use**;
- (b) the fact that these values have been published, with the source cited, in a public source-code repository (GitHub) since 22 November 2026;
- (c) the form of attribution you would like us to use.

The tables themselves will not be distributed; only the aggregate values listed above will be used.
