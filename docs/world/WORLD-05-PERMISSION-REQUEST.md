## 1. What is requested

Permission to reproduce and distribute, **including for commercial purposes**, with attribution:
1. **Fiscal statistics:** about 20 general government aggregates for 2023 from the Ministry's *General Government Sector Fiscal Statistics (GFSM 2014)*, and the defence row of the *2023 central government budget outturn by function and economic type*. The defence row includes **personnel expenditure** and **state social security contributions**, which feed Rule W (military personnel expenditure).
2. **Debt:** the end-2023 EU-defined general government debt ratio from the *Public Debt Management Report*. The report is marked "© … All Rights Reserved. May not be copied or duplicated, in whole or in part without reference".
3. **Derived data:** ratios to GDP and calibrated model values derived from them, in versioned game scenario files.
4. **Existing publication:** confirmation that figures from the Court of Accounts' *2023 Fiscal Statistics Evaluation Report* and from the debt report may stay in the public GitHub repository where they have been since 22 November 2026. The repository holds aggregate figures only, no documents.
5. **Future commercial redistribution** of the game and its data files, free or paid.
6. **The attribution wording** the authority wants, and whether any further condition applies.

**How to send (the owner decides):**
1. **Fill in** the sender's name, contact details and the game's public name, then sign. Keep the item lists and the attribution question as written.
2. **Choose a channel:**
   - **CİMER**, the Presidency's Communication Centre for applications to public bodies, is the most traceable. It normally needs an e-Devlet login, so check whether that is available to the sender.
   - Otherwise send a **signed letter by registered post** to the addresses above.
   - For letter B, also send it **by e-mail** to the address printed in the report.
3. **Keep copies** of what was sent and of the replies. Record the date, channel and any reference number in `docs/world/data/world05-licence-evidence.json`, for example through a future task. Only a written permission, not silence, clears the source.
4. **Follow up.** Applications under the Right to Information Law (No. 4982) are answered within 15 working days, or 30 (Art. 11). A permission request may fall outside that law, so treat the deadline as indicative and follow up once after about a month.

**This document does not provide legal advice.** The owner may want a lawyer to review the wording.

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am [name], developer of *[game name]*, a strategy game played in a web browser. Its European scenario takes each country's 2023 starting values from official statistics.

- (b) the publication, with the source cited and since 22 November 2026, of some aggregate figures from the Court of Accounts' *2023 Fiscal Statistics Evaluation Report* and from the *Public Debt Management Report* in a public source-code repository (GitHub);
- (c) your preferred attribution and any further conditions.

The tables and reports themselves will not be distributed.
